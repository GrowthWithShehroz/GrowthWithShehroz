Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Islamic Daily Wisdom mobile application ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and shares information about you.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Location — only when you tap Detect location in Settings. Latitude and longitude are used to compute prayer times. Coordinates are stored on your device.

1.2 Information generated by the App

  • Anonymous user ID — Firebase Authentication assigns a random UID on first launch. No name, email, phone, or other personal identifier is collected.
  • Prayer completion history — when you mark prayers complete in the Prayers tab, the entries are stored on your device and mirrored to your anonymous Firestore document so you can recover your streak after reinstall.
  • App usage events — Firebase Analytics records anonymized events (screen views, taps on share / upgrade) to help us improve the App.

1.3 Information collected by third parties

  • Google AdMob — serves banner and interstitial ads. May collect a resettable advertising ID and IP address. See: Google Ads policies.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — shows a consent form on first launch in regions that require it (EEA / UK). Your consent choice is stored by the consent SDK on your device.
  • RevenueCat — processes subscription receipts when you purchase Premium. Receives a hashed user ID, your purchase token, and product ID. See: RevenueCat Privacy.

2. How we use your information

  • Compute accurate prayer times for your location.
  • Send local notifications at prayer times you have enabled.
  • Track your prayer streak.
  • Validate Premium subscription receipts.
  • Serve ads to non-Premium users.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App.

3. How we share your information

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication, analytics, and storing your anonymous prayer history.
  • AdMob (Google) — for ad serving.
  • RevenueCat — for subscription validation.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

  • On-device data persists until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Anonymous Firestore data is removed immediately when you tap Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data.
  • Analytics events are retained per Google Firebase's default retention (currently 14 months).

5. Children

The App is rated for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • Location — toggle off in your device's system settings or revoke the permission.
  • Notifications — toggle individual prayers in the Prayers tab, or revoke at the system level.
  • Personalized ads — change your preference in the consent form (re-show by reinstalling the App), or in your device's Ad Settings.
  • Subscription — manage at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data.

7. Security

We use the security features provided by Firebase, AdMob, and Google Play. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your anonymous data, please contact: privacy@islamicdailywisdom.app (placeholder — update before publishing).
