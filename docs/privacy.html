Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Islamic Daily Wisdom is fully free. No advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions. We do not earn revenue from this app — it is offered for the spiritual benefit of the Muslim community (sadaqah jariyah).

This Privacy Policy describes how the Islamic Daily Wisdom mobile application ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and shares information about you.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Location — only when you tap Detect location in Settings. Latitude and longitude are used to compute prayer times. Coordinates are stored on your device only.

1.2 Information generated by the App

  • Anonymous user ID — Firebase Authentication assigns a random UID on first launch. No name, email, phone, or other personal identifier is collected.
  • Prayer completion history — when you mark prayers complete, the entries are stored on your device and mirrored to your anonymous Firestore document so you can recover your streak after reinstall.
  • App usage events — Firebase Analytics records anonymized events to help us improve the App.

1.3 Information collected by third parties

  • Firebase (Google) — provides anonymous authentication, Firestore database, and Analytics. Subject to Google's privacy terms.

There are no advertising SDKs, no in-app purchase SDKs, and no other third-party trackers in this app.

2. How we use your information

  • Compute accurate prayer times for your location.
  • Send local notifications at prayer times you have enabled.
  • Track your prayer streak.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App.

3. How we share your information

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication, analytics, and storing your anonymous prayer history.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or any other third parties.

4. Data retention

  • On-device data persists until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Anonymous Firestore data is removed immediately when you tap Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data.
  • Analytics events are retained per Google Firebase's default retention (currently 14 months).

5. Children

The App is rated for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • Location — toggle off in your device's system settings or revoke the permission.
  • Notifications — toggle individual prayers in the Prayers tab, or revoke at the system level.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data.

7. Security

We use the security features provided by Firebase and Google Play. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your anonymous data, please contact: Shehroz@growthwithshehroz.com.
